## 📌 Overview
This repository contains an interactive analytics dashboard designed to monitor and evaluate insurance risk and claim patterns. It provides comprehensive insights into policyholder demographics, vehicle specifications, and coverage zones to help identify key drivers of claim amounts and frequencies.

The dashboard UI was intentionally designed using a **Monochromatic Muted Purple/Mauve** theme, moving away from harsh neon colors to provide a modern, professional, and eye-friendly analytical experience.

## 📊 Key Metrics & KPIs
- **Total Policies:** 37,542
- **Total Claim Amount:** $187.8M
- **Average Claim Frequency:** 0.51
- **Average Claim Amount:** $5.0K
- **Demographic Split:** Balanced distribution between Male (18.7K) and Female (18.8K) policyholders.

## 🔍 Key Visualizations & Insights
- **Vehicle Use & Make:** Breakdown of claim amounts comparing Private vs. Commercial use, highlighting top manufacturers (Ford, Chevrolet) by claim volume.
- **Coverage Zone Risk:** Financial impact distribution across Urban, Rural, and Suburban territories.
- **Demographic Risk Factors:** Analysis of claim amounts segmented by **Age Group** (implemented via custom DAX grouping), **Education Level**, and **Marital Status**.
- **Historical Trends:** Time-series analysis of claim amounts based on the **Car Year** (1990-2020).
- **Family Impact:** Evaluation of the correlation between the number of kids driving and total claim amounts.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **BI Tool:** Power BI *(Ubah jika Anda menggunakan Tableau/Looker)*
- **Data Processing:** Data Modeling, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for custom columns and measures.
- **Design & UI/UX:** Custom Monochromatic Muted Mauve palette tailored for professional data presentation and reduced visual fatigue.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open with **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the filter panel/slicers on the top right to slice the data by specific measures (e.g., Total Claim Amount).
